01Scope

This policy covers the ZAT POS mobile app (Android package com.zatpos.app), its desktop counterpart, and the ZAT POS sync service at zaterp.com that the app connects to. ZAT POS is a point-of-sale application built for merchants operating in Saudi Arabia, including support for ZATCA-compliant e-invoicing.

ZAT POS is a business tool used by the merchant (you) and your staff. It is not directed at, or intended for use by, consumers browsing or shopping — the people whose data appears in the app are your own customers and transactions, which you control as the data controller for your business.

02Data we collect

ZAT POS is offline-first: everything below is stored on your device first, and synced to ZAT POS servers over an encrypted connection whenever the device is online and signed in to a business account.

03Payment method records

When you complete a sale, ZAT POS records the payment method you select for your own bookkeeping (for example, "cash" or "card"). The app does not integrate a card reader or payment gateway, and does not capture, transmit, or store full card numbers or other live card data.

If a card reader or payment gateway integration is added in the future, this section — and the corresponding Google Play Data Safety declaration — will need to be updated to name the processor and describe how card data is handled.

04How we use it

  • Operate core point-of-sale functionality: sales, inventory, customers, receipts.
  • Keep your data in sync across every device and location on your account.
  • Generate ZATCA-compliant e-invoices where required by Saudi tax regulation.
  • Authenticate your devices and protect your account from unauthorized access.

We do not use your business or customer data to serve ads, and we do not build advertising profiles from it.

05Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Data is shared only in these cases:

  • Saudi tax authority (ZATCA) — e-invoice data is submitted where legally required for e-invoicing compliance.
  • Infrastructure providers — hosting and server infrastructure that stores and syncs your account data on our behalf, under contract, and only to provide the service.
  • Legal requirements — if required to comply with a valid legal process under Saudi law.

06Storage & security

  • Data in transit to ZAT POS servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Data at rest on your device is stored in the app's private storage, inaccessible to other apps.
  • Android's automatic device backup is disabled for ZAT POS, so business and account data is never copied to a Google account backup.
  • Local printer communication is limited to your local network and is not routed through ZAT POS servers.

07Retention & deletion

Business and transaction records are retained for as long as your account is active, and afterward as required by Saudi commercial and tax recordkeeping rules. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us — we'll retain only what the law requires us to keep, and delete the rest.

08Your rights

Under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you or, as the merchant, about your customers. Contact us at info@zaterp.com to make a request.

09Children

ZAT POS is a business tool for merchants and staff. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children through the app.

10Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify account owners in-app or by email.
